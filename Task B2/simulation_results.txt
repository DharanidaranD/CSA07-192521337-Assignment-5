Expected (theory) to compare with: with FIFO the 1,500 B download packets queue ahead of 200 B voice packets; on a 2 Mbps link a
full queue of 20 x 1,500 B adds 20 x 6 ms = 120 ms delay (and drops) -> voice becomes "poor". With priority queuing the voice
waits at most one packet in service (1,500 B = 6 ms at 2 Mbps), keeping delay low while download takes the leftover bandwidth.
